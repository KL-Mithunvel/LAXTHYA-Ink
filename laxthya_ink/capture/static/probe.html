Signal Probe

Draw with the Apple Pencil.

Do these, in order

  1. Hold the Pencil just above the screen and move it, without touching.
  2. Write a few words in your normal hand.
  3. Draw a line pressing lightly, then hard.
  4. Tilt the Pencil flat, then upright, while drawing.
  5. Rotate (roll) the Pencil barrel while it touches the screen.
  6. Squeeze the Pencil Pro barrel.
  7. Press Send to laptop.
